Privacy notice

SnapWorth is operated by Zycon Studios. This notice describes the data used by the application.

What the app processes

Accounts contain an email address, a password hash and session information. Saved items contain the details you enter, estimates, notes and any saved photo. Uploads used for identification are resized and stripped of image metadata before being sent to OpenAI. Do not upload photos containing information you do not want processed, such as private documents or identifiable bystanders.

Why and where

These data enable sign-in, image identification, marketplace search and your collection. OpenAI processes identification images. eBay receives search terms for comparable listings. FINN, Facebook and other search links open their own services when you choose them. Application and session records are stored in the configured PostgreSQL database and processed by the hosting provider.

Storage and control

Saved account and item records remain until deleted. You can delete individual items or your account in Account using your current password. Account deletion removes its saved items from the active database. Session cookies last up to 30 days; signing out invalidates the current session. Local scan history stays on the current device and is cleared when you sign out. Daily quota counters prevent abuse.

Provider logs and backups may retain data separately from the active database. Contact support for information about the retention periods that apply to backups and provider logs. An identification request uses OpenAI's storage opt-out, but this does not mean the provider retains no data under its own policies.

Contact and requests

Use the support address on the Contact page for privacy questions or requests you cannot complete in the app.
